# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Muslim users seeking a calm, dependable daily companion for Quran reading,
prayer times, hadith, dua, dhikr, and other everyday worship routines. The
product is designed for an international audience rather than a single mosque
or locality.

## Product Purpose

Hudhud brings essential Islamic practices and references into one focused,
approachable mobile experience. Success means users can reach the right daily
practice quickly and return with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Warm, wise, and trustworthy. Hudhud should feel quietly uplifting and modern,
with the sense of direction and knowledge carried by the hoopoe symbol.

## Anti-references

Avoid single-mosque institutional branding, ornamental religious pastiche,
overly ceremonial interfaces, generic finance-style luxury, and playful visuals
that weaken the dignity of worship content.

## Design Principles

- Put daily worship tasks ahead of decoration.
- Express Islamic identity through respectful, restrained visual cues.
- Keep navigation familiar and content easy to scan.
- Use warmth to welcome users without reducing clarity or contrast.
- Make every state feel dependable, calm, and intentional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, scalable text, clear semantics, comfortable touch
targets, and reduced-motion support. Do not rely on color alone to communicate
meaning.
